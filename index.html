Education Loan EMI Calculator with Moratorium — Full Moratorium vs Paying Interest During Study

Generic EMI tools skip the years when interest quietly accrues and nobody pays. eduloan models the moratorium the way the cited IBA model scheme describes it — simple interest through course + 12 months, then EMI on the capitalised balance — and compares pay nothing, pay the interest, and pay a little to the paisa.

      • Moratorium interest follows the cited IBA model scheme (simple interest); banks may compound monthly or set their own rules. The toggle covers the two common treatments — figures may still differ from your bank's statement.
